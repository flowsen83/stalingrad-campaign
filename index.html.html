Official VP milestones

Germany: +1 order die at 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 VP. At 50 VP, the Volga freezes: draws count as German victories.

Soviet Union: +1 order die at 10, 20 and 30 VP. At 30 VP, Germany may no longer field Forward Air Observers. At 70 VP, Operation Uranus can win the campaign.

The victory check occurs at the end of the turn. If both sides meet their conditions, Germany wins. For campaign map eligibility and scenario-specific rules, refer to the campaign book.
